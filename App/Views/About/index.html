About

An open chat room on a map

Everyone who signs in talks in one shared room.
A pin on the map shows the city each person is chatting from.

I built it in 2018 as a university project at Boise State University. The goal was to understand what a web framework actually does, so instead of using one, I wrote my own: the router, controllers, models, views, sessions and login are all hand-written PHP.

In 2026 I came back to it to bring parts of it up to date.

{{ icon('github') }}The code is open source on GitHub

What you can do

{{ icon('chat-round-dots') }}Chatting

  • Chat with everyone who is signed in, in one shared room.
  • Click someone's name to chat privately, and see when they're typing to you.
  • Type @ to mention someone. Mentions of you are highlighted.
  • A calm ping for a private message or mention in a chat you aren't looking at.
  • Add emoji, or type one the old way: :-) turns into 🙂. Press the mic to speak a message and check it before sending.
  • Pick From and To with the translate button, and your message is sent in that language. Others can tap See original.
  • Get notified of private messages and mentions, even with MapChat closed.

{{ icon('map-point') }}The map

  • See where each person online is chatting from, and their local time.
  • Click a person or a message's city to fly to their pin.
  • Where it's night is shaded.
  • Hide yourself from the map whenever you want.
  • On a phone, the chat sits over the map. Drag it to the height you like.

{{ icon('stars-minimalistic') }}AI in the room

  • MapChat, the room's AI, chats in the room or privately, and says hello when it's quiet.
  • Some people on the map are AI characters. Mention or message one and they answer.

{{ icon('user') }}Your account

  • Try it as a guest. Guest accounts go after 24 hours unless you keep them.
  • Continue with Google instead of a password, where the site has it set up.
  • A profile with your name, an avatar and, if you like, a nickname.
  • Delete your profile any time, with your messages and pin.

How it's built

{{ icon('programming') }}Built from scratch

  • A model-view-controller (MVC) framework written from scratch in PHP.
  • MySQL, queried with prepared statements to block SQL injection.
  • Twig templates, which escape everything users type to block cross-site scripting.

{{ icon('plain') }}Live chat

  • A WebSocket per open chat, on amphp, so messages and who's online or typing arrive at once.
  • Without it, the chat checks every second, or every 8 in a hidden tab.
  • Web Push and a service worker, so notifications arrive with the page closed.

{{ icon('map') }}Map and AI

  • MapLibre and OpenStreetMap for the map.
  • Groq writes what the AI says, or Cerebras when Groq is busy. Groq's Whisper writes out voice messages.

{{ icon('eye') }}Look and feel

  • The site's own CSS, with Beer CSS for the form fields, and plain JavaScript with no jQuery.
  • Icons from Heroicons by Tailwind Labs, under the MIT licence.

Good to know

{{ icon('info-circle') }}Before you chat

  • Your location comes from your IP address, rounded to about a kilometre. You can hide your pin and city from your profile.
  • Anything you write in Everyone is visible to everyone signed in. Private chats are only shown to the two of you.
  • MapChat and some people on the map are AI. Groq writes their replies, or Cerebras when Groq is busy.
  • Messages you translate go to Groq, or to Cerebras when Groq is busy.
  • Voice messages go to Groq to be written out, or to your browser's speech service when it's busy. The recording isn't kept.
  • This is a portfolio project, so please don't share anything private here.
  • What MapChat keeps, and who else sees it, is in the privacy policy.
